Program overview

Learning through discovery

Science, technology, engineering, art, and math connected to environmental themes.

EcoScience Learning is currently presented as a proposed educational initiative. The experiences below are examples of the direction of the program, rather than confirmed bookable services.

Learning goals

What participants can explore

Investigate a question

Use observation and experiments to explore a real-world environmental topic.

Design and create

Build models, record ideas, and test possible solutions.

Reflect and share

Compare observations, explain results, and communicate what was learned.

Proposed format

Possible activities

  • Classroom-friendly activities that encourage curiosity
  • Connections between scientific ideas and creative problem-solving
  • Potential adaptation for different ages and learning environments
Illustrative example

Example kit concept: Water and filtration

Learners can compare materials, construct simple filter models, observe results, and discuss what the activity teaches about water quality. This is a concept, not a commercial kit listing.
