Our purpose

Environmental education through experience

EcoScience Learning is a proposed initiative focused on environmental education, hands-on science, and STEAM experiences for young learners and communities.

The concept is to encourage students to explore the natural world, develop critical thinking, and connect scientific knowledge with meaningful action.

This demonstration website communicates the project's vision and potential program directions. It does not imply that services are currently operating, that partnerships have been established, or that activities are available for booking.
