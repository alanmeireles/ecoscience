Program overview

Learning through discovery

Outdoor learning, science inquiry, and environmental awareness.

EcoScience Learning is currently presented as a proposed educational initiative. The experiences below are examples of the direction of the program, rather than confirmed bookable services.

Learning goals

What participants can explore

Explore ecosystems

Observe relationships between living things and the environments they depend on.

Understand biodiversity

Investigate why variety in plants, animals, and habitats matters.

Practice sustainable thinking

Connect everyday decisions with practical environmental stewardship.

Proposed format

Possible activities

  • Guided observations and age-appropriate nature investigations
  • Simple experiments, discussions, and reflection activities
  • Flexible concepts for classroom or community learning
Illustrative example

Example workshop theme: Explore a local habitat

Students can observe a nearby habitat, record what they notice, ask questions about plants and animals, and discuss ways to care for the space.
